### Ingredients
---
**Chicken**
- 4 [[chicken thighs]] (boneless/skinless preferred; bone-in OK)
- 1 tsp [[kosher salt]]
- 1/2 tsp [[black pepper]]
- 1 tsp [[garlic powder]] *(optional)*

**Sauce**
- 3 tbsp [[butter]] (divided)
- 1 tbsp [[olive oil]] *(optional)*
- 1 medium [[onion]] (finely diced) *(optional but recommended)*
- 4 cloves [[garlic]] (minced)
- 1 tbsp [[ginger]] (grated) *(or 1 tsp ground ginger)*
- 2 cups (480 g) [[tomato sauce]] (garden sauce) *(use up to 4 cups / 2 pints for a saucier batch)*
- 3/4 cup (180 g) [[heavy cream]]
- 1 tsp [[garam masala]]
- 2 tsp [[ground cumin]]
- 2 tsp [[ground coriander]]
- 1 tsp [[paprika]] *(smoked ok, but regular is more classic)*
- 1/2 tsp [[turmeric]]
- 1/4 tsp [[cayenne pepper]] *(optional; adjust heat)*
- 1 tsp [[kosher salt]] (start with 1/2 tsp if your sauce is salted)
- 1 tbsp [[lemon juice]] *(or 2 tsp vinegar; add at end)*
- 1 tsp [[monkfruit/allulose sweetener]] *(optional; if sauce is very acidic)*
- Optional: 2 tbsp [[parmesan cheese]] *(not traditional; adds body if needed)*
- Optional garnish: [[cilantro]]

### Directions
1. **Brown the thighs.** Pat thighs dry. Season with salt/pepper (and garlic powder if using). Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Add 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp oil. Sear thighs 3–4 min per side until browned. Remove to a plate (not cooked through).
2. **Build the base.** Lower heat to medium. Add remaining butter. Add onion and cook 5–7 min until soft. Add garlic + ginger, cook 30 sec.
3. **Bloom spices (don’t skip).** Add garam masala, cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric, cayenne. Stir 30–60 sec until fragrant.
4. **Simmer the tomato sauce.** Add garden tomato sauce. Simmer 10–15 min until it tastes “cooked” and slightly thickened. (If your sauce is thin, simmer longer.)
5. **Blend smooth (restaurant move).** Use an immersion blender until smooth (or blend carefully in a blender and return).
6. **Add cream gently.** Lower heat to low. Stir in heavy cream. Keep it at a gentle simmer (no hard boil).
7. **Finish cooking chicken in sauce.** Return thighs (and juices) to pan. Cover and simmer:
   - boneless: 10–15 min
   - bone-in: 20–30 min  
   Until chicken is tender and done.
8. **Final balance.** Turn off heat. Stir in lemon juice. Taste.
   - Too acidic: add 1 tsp sweetener and/or 1 tbsp butter.
   - Too flat: add a pinch of salt + 1/2 tsp garam masala.
   - Too thick: splash of water/broth.
9. **Serve.** Great over cauliflower rice. Top with cilantro.

### Notes
- **Using the full 2 pints:** double the spices and cream, or keep spices the same for a milder, more tomato-forward batch.
- **Make-ahead:** better next day. Reheat gently.
